[y452] (x59)Electronic Protection (overheating)
[y473] (x59)The module has integrated electronic protection against overheating. This is activated when the
[y486] (x59)temperature of the laser diode exceeds the factory preset maximum temperature of the laser diode.
[y499] (x59)The switch (x112)- (x116)on and switch (x185)- (x189)off process of the electronic protection is subject to a hysteresis with a
[y511] (x59)temperature difference of 5 Kelvin and is used for the cooling phase. If the temperature falls below the
[y524] (x59)switch (x90)- (x95)on temperature after the electronic protection is triggered, the module switches on again
[y537] (x59)automatically.
