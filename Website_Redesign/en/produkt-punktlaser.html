Your benefits

Flexible manufacturing – often at no extra cost

We manufacture dot laser modules based on numerous common laser diodes – with the right optics and electronics for your task.

Custom housings, specified laser diodes, special beam profiles, divergence and spot size can often be realized at no additional cost. You can specify requirements for beam direction errors, temperature drift and electronic properties.

In addition to a wide range of standard dot lasers, we also develop modules to your specifications in small series.

High beam quality

Round or elliptical beam profiles with long-term stability.

Customized

Housing, laser diode, divergence and spot size to your specifications.

Easy to integrate into devices and systems.
