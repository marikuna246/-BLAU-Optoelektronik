Your benefits

Reproducible laser lines in the top quality segment

For patient positioning, industrial positioning systems, welding systems and metrology – wherever a stable, clearly defined line is required.

As standard, the line lasers feature a fixed focus and a robust 11.5 × 55 mm housing. Compact form factors from 8 × 10 mm and versions with mirror deflection are optionally available.

Depending on the application, we generate the line with cylindrical lenses (Gaussian intensity), raster lenses (line ends of nearly equal brightness) or diffractive optical elements (DOE).

High beam quality

Line projection in the top quality segment.

Defined laser class

Classification as class 1, 1M, 2, 2M or 3R according to your safety requirements.

Flexible integration

Digital and analog modulation up to 1 MHz, including dimming options.
