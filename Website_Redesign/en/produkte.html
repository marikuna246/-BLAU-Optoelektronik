Products

Laser modules and optics for your application

13 products in five categories. On request, we adapt many parameters – such as wavelength, power, optics and housing – to your task.

Machine Vision: Line lasers for industrial machine vision. They project a fine, homogeneous laser line that is evaluated by a camera – e.g. for 3D measurement, profile and surface inspection.

Line lasers: High-quality laser lines and beam patterns for positioning and measurement tasks – in medical technology, industry and metrology.

Dot lasers: Laser modules with a round beam profile – optics and electronics to suit your application.

Powell lenses: Special optics that fan a laser beam out into a line of uniform brightness. Made in-house.

OEM modules: Laser modules built to your specifications – mechanics, optics and electronics, even in small series.
