Your benefits

What a Powell lens does

Powell lenses are aspherical cylindrical lenses. They transform a Gaussian-distributed laser beam into a line with homogeneous intensity – a prerequisite for many camera-based measurement systems.

Our manufacturing process includes optical design, simulation, CNC-based grinding and polishing, as well as laser-based inspection of every single lens.

This enables us to manufacture custom Powell lenses as well: adapted to the input distribution of your laser beam or for a desired distribution at the lens output.

Homogeneous laser lines

Uniform intensity from a Gaussian laser beam.

Consistent quality

In-house production with laser-based inspection of every lens.

Customized

Tailored input and output beam profiles on request.
