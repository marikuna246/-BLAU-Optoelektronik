Your benefits

Designed for series production

The MVsquare is primarily intended for the series production of 3D systems in industrial image processing.

Focus, beam position and all other parameters are adjusted during manufacturing according to your specifications. You mount the module without additional alignment – and the settings remain stable in the finished product.

For particularly space-saving installation, the MVsquare is available on request with the laser beam deflected by 90°, exiting laterally from the housing.

Factory-adjusted

No alignment needed in the system – this saves time in your assembly.

Superior line quality

Multiple focus and optics options for your ratio of line width to depth of field.

Reduces Z-axis footprint

Particularly flat installation with 90° beam deflection.

Technical data

Technical data at a glance

Focus options

They determine the ratio of line width to depth of field. Each option can be combined with all fan angles.

  • STD · Standard
  • DL · thin line
  • DLE · thin line, enhanced
  • DLSE · thin line, super enhanced
  • TS · extended depth of field
  • TS2 · extended depth of field, enhanced

Fan angle

The fan angle determines the line length at the respective working distance.

  • 1°
  • 5°
  • 10°
  • 20°
  • 30°
  • 45°
  • 60°
  • 75°
  • 90°

60°, 75° and 90° only available for R0R0, R0R90 and R90R0.
