Your benefits

A clean line – even after years in operation

The focusing mechanism has been fundamentally redesigned in the 2025 edition.

The modules are now reliably protected against dust, splash water and moisture ingress, and beam deviation during focusing has been reduced to a minimum.

Is your existing system designed for the previous design? No problem: the MVnano is still available with the older mechanical variant and the familiar dimensions.

Homogeneous laser line

The Powell lens from our own production ensures uniform brightness across the entire line – at all wavelengths.

Multiple optics options

Choose the right ratio of line width to depth of field for your application.

Robust focusing

Protected against dust, splash water and moisture – with minimal beam deviation.

Housing variants

Three designs for your installation space

Each available with adjustable or factory-set focus.

FP-MVnano-25-ST

Standard

Optics and electronics in a single housing.

FP-MVnano-25-2H

Two housings

Optics and electronics separated – particularly space-saving.

FP-MVnano-25-OH

Optical head

Optics without electronics, for installation in camera systems. The laser driver must be provided by the customer.
