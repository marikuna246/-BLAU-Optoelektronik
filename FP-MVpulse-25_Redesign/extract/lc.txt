[y337] (x59)INCREASED PEAK POWER (UP TO 5 TIMES) WITHIN THE SAME LASER CLASS.
[y356] (x59)High light intensity and therefore high laser power is a key factor in various machine vision applications.
[y370] (x59)However, when using laser light, obeying laser class regulations is sometimes a limiting factor.
[y390] (x59)Laser manufacturers classify their lasers in continuous-wave operation, which, in combination with a
[y404] (x59)camera, is not the most commonly used operating mode. The pulse control can align the laser signals
[y418] (x59)with the camera, so that up to 5-times higher peak power (compared to cw mode) can be achieved
[y432] (x59)while staying in the same laser class.
[y452] (x59)The MVpulse (2025) is available in 5 wavelengths (450 – 785 nm) at peak powers up to 100 mW. (x528)Various
[y466] (x59)fan angles (1–90°) are available to achieve the required line length, and six different optical options
[y480] (x59)provide a suitable combination of line width and depth of field for your application. The modules come
[y494] (x59)with fixed or adjustable focus in different housings: Standard or 2H (two separate housings - one for the
[y508] (x59)optics with the laser diode and the other for the electronics).
