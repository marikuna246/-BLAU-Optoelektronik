About us

Laser modules from Überlingen on Lake Constance – since 1987

We are a medium-sized manufacturer of laser modules and optics. Development, prototyping and series production all come from a single source.

1987founded
2019start of in-house Powell lens production
ISO 9001certified to DIN EN ISO 9001:2015
100 %development & production in Germany

What we stand for

Precise laser technology – tailored to your application

Our customers use lasers where accuracy matters: in X-ray equipment, inspection systems, robots and laboratory setups. That is why we develop our products in close collaboration with them – and adapt them when the standard version is not enough.

Made in Germany is more than a promise of quality – it stands for value creation in our region and regional supply chains throughout the DACH region.

History

Milestones

From laser modules for medical technology to line lasers for industrial machine vision.

  1. 1987

    Foundation

    Founded with a focus on laser modules for medical technology.

  2. 2000

    Industrial machine vision

    Portfolio expanded to include solutions for machine vision.

  3. 2010

    Proprietary sensor technology

    Development of proprietary optical sensor technologies and PSD sensors. In the same year, we began selling a portion of our laser modules directly.

  4. 2019

    In-house Powell lens production

    Start of in-house production – today several hundred lenses per week.

  5. 2023

    MVpulse

    Launch of the MVpulse: high power at laser class 2.
