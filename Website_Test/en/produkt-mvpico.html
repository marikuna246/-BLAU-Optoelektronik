Your benefits

High performance despite compact size

The MVpico features the new focusing mechanism of the 2025 edition: you can adjust the focus distance faster and more accurately.

Various optics variants offer the best ratio between line width and depth of field for every application.

Available with adjustable focus, with factory-fixed focus and with separated optics and electronics – with and without drive electronics. Other requirements? Contact us.

Compact

Ø 10 × 50 mm – ideal for integration into 3D sensors.

Focusing, 2025 edition

Superior line quality

Multiple focus and optics options.
