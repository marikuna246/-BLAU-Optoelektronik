Your benefits

Shorter exposure, more parts per second

More light allows shorter exposure times. This lets you capture more images per second and inspect more parts in less time.

Up to 1,000 mW at 525, 660 and 808 nm and up to 2,000 mW at 445 nm. An integrated thermoelectric cooler (TEC) prevents overheating and ensures stable operation.

Two M12 connectors, control via RS-232 (0–5 V, standard) or optionally RS-485, and digital modulation (active high or active low) simplify integration into existing systems.
